Major Arcana: The Soul’s Journey

The Major Arcana cards represent life’s big lessons and spiritual milestones. Click below to explore each card’s unique wisdom!

  1. The Magician (Le Bateleur) - Mastery and manifestation
  2. The High Priestess (La Pances) - Intuition and mystery
  3. The Empress (Imperatris) - Abundance and nurturing
  4. The Emperor (L'Empereur) - Authority and structure
  5. The Hierophant (Le Pape) - Tradition and guidance
  6. The Lovers (L'Amoureu) - Connection and choice
  7. The Chariot (Le Charior) - Determination and victory
  8. Justice - Fairness and balance
  9. The Hermit (L'Ermite) - Introspection and wisdom
  10. The Wheel of Fortune (La Roue de Fortun) - Cycles and destiny
  11. Strength (Force) - Courage and inner power
  12. The Hanged Man (Le Pandu) - Surrender and perspective
  13. Death - Transformation and endings
  14. Temperance - Balance and harmony
  15. The Devil (Le Diable) - Temptation and liberation
  16. The Tower (La Maison Dieu) - Sudden change
  17. The Star (L'etoille) - Hope and inspiration
  18. The Moon (La Lune) - Intuition and dreams
  19. The Sun (Le Soleil) - Joy and success
  20. Judgement (Le Jugement) - Awakening and renewal
  21. The Fool (Le Fol) - New beginnings (0 or 21)
  22. The World (Lemonde) - Completion (21 or 0)
